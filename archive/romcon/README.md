# Love Season

Love Season is a romance life sim about navigating an eight-week reality-courtship season.

You balance private connection, public image, rivalry, and long-term compatibility before the finale decides what the season was really worth.

## Gameplay

- Choose scenes and social opportunities.
- Spend time with different cast members.
- Weigh private romance against public perception.
- Manage rivalry, compatibility, and reputation.
- Prepare for the finale across eight weeks.

## Goal

Build a relationship strong enough to survive the season while keeping your public story from falling apart.

## Controls

- Mouse: choose scenes and use interface actions.

## Current Scope

Playable courtship structure with scene choices, social pressure, rivalries, compatibility, and season progression.
