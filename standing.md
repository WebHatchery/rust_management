# Game Standing

Scope: active top-level game projects only. Archived projects under `archive/`, shared tooling such as `macroquad-toolkit`, and starter templates are excluded.

Last updated: 2026-07-26

Criteria: ranked by MVP scale and apparent implementation complexity, mainly Rust code size, system count, data/assets, and how much game state the project appears to manage. This is not a fun/polish ranking. LOC/file counts below are a straight recount (`*.rs` under each project's `src/`, plus any sibling crates in the same repo — `mytherra` and `kaiju_sim`) taken fresh this pass, so figures may differ slightly from earlier snapshots even where nothing changed — treat drift within a few percent as noise, not movement.

**Movement this pass was extremely concentrated.** Only four projects have commits since 2026-07-21: `mytherra` (136), `stellar_legacy` (290), `dragons_hoard` (54, brand new), and `dragons_den` (1, a copy edit). The other 24 games are byte-for-byte unchanged since the shared-web-shell migration, and their recounts confirm it — so their entries below are carried forward verbatim and their relative order is unchanged; they only shift rank to make room at the top. But those four commits' worth of work is enormous: `stellar_legacy` grew 4.7×, `mytherra` was rebuilt into a networked client/server system, and `dragons_hoard` arrived at ~30k LOC from nothing. All three now sit at or above what was the largest codebase in the catalog, so the whole top of the table is reshuffled and `dungeon_manager` drops from 1st to 4th without changing a line.

**Finish estimates:** each game now carries a rough estimate of how long a full-time team of 4 (mixed engineering / art / design-audio, or generalists managing outsourced assets) would need to take the current codebase to a finished, shippable 1.0 — content-complete, balanced, QA'd, with **all placeholder/procedural artwork replaced by commissioned art and a full sound/music pass**. Estimates are calendar months, dominated as much by asset volume (creature rosters, card illustrations, animation sets) as by remaining code. `dragons_hoard` is the one deliberate exception to the art clause — its visuals and music are generated in code by design, not placeholders awaiting replacement, and its estimate reflects that. Treat them all as order-of-magnitude planning numbers, not commitments.
